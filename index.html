A proposed common operating geography for the human settlement of Mars: a persistent area grid (MGRID), a deterministic binary place address (BAR), and a possible three-word human interface (Triplet).

One planet. A geography people can operate.

The illustrated edition includes 22 chapters across five parts, nine appendices and the Map Room. It explains how machine-readable Mars place identities can coexist with scientific mapping, future local names, property boundaries and cultural geography.

This is an explanatory publication. The MRR-1 external reference verification, full physical BAR conformance and production Triplet vocabulary remain separate technical work; the website does not certify them.
